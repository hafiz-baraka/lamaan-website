The full system

What we clean, from hood to roof

Grease does not stop at the filters. It travels up the whole exhaust system, so a proper clean covers every part.

  1. 1
    Exhaust fan & motorBlades and housing load up with grease and lose suction.
  2. 2
    DuctworkHidden from view, and the main path for a fire to spread.
  3. 3
    Hood canopyInside walls and the space behind the filters.
  4. 4
    Baffle filtersThe first line of defence. Removed, soaked and scrubbed.
  5. 5
    Grease cup & gutterEmptied and cleaned so grease cannot overflow onto the cooking line.

Cleaning schedule

How often should your hood be cleaned?

International fire-safety guidance (NFPA 96) sets the interval by how hard your kitchen works. Use this as a starting point; we confirm the right schedule after inspecting your system.

Type of kitchenClean everyVisits in a year
Charcoal or wood-fired cookingMonth
Shawarma, grills, 24-hour kitchens, wok cooking3 months
Typical restaurants and cafeterias6 months
Low-volume or seasonal kitchens12 months
