About us

Lamaan Al-Khaleej Cleaning Services

A leading Emirati company based in Abu Dhabi, specialising in integrated, high-quality cleaning solutions. The company was founded on a commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction, delivering professional services that rely on the latest techniques and best practices in the sector.

We believe cleanliness is not just appearance; it is a way of life that reflects quality of work and attention to detail. That is why we dedicate our efforts to giving our clients a clean, healthy environment, at home or at work.

Our vision

To be the number one choice for cleaning services in the UAE, through exceptional quality and unmatched professionalism.

Our mission

To provide clients with a clean and healthy environment through reliable, fast and efficient cleaning services, while ensuring the highest safety and hygiene standards.

Booking FAQs

    

      
How do I book a cleaning service?
Choose a service above, fill in the three steps of the booking form and send it on WhatsApp. You can also message or call us directly.

      
How much does it cost?
It depends on the service, the size of the space and its condition. Send us a few details or photos on WhatsApp and we will send you a quote.

      
Do you work at night?
Yes. For restaurants and offices we can schedule the work after closing time, so you do not lose any business hours.

      
Which areas do you cover?
We are based in Abu Dhabi and serve homes, businesses and institutions across the emirate. Message us with your location.

      
Can I sign a yearly contract?
Yes. We offer monthly and annual contracts for companies and institutions, plus quarterly and semi-annual plans for kitchens, all with fixed visit dates.
